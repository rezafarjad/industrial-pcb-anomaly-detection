# Code of conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation from people of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and technically honest.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Give credit for data, research, code, and reproduced results.
- Protect private production images, credentials, and vulnerability details.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, spam, and publication of another person's
private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this policy
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Platform-level
abuse should also be reported through GitHub's reporting tools. Confidential
project-specific concerns may be submitted through the private reporting
channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
